Is my data safe with an AI agent?The agent reads only what your role can already see, and every write it proposes needs your approval. Salary and bank data are encrypted, and platform staff cannot browse your payroll — support access is time-boxed, reasoned and written to your audit log.
Do I have to leave Tally or Busy?No. Keep them and take the export, or move payroll accounting into Emplora and let your CA pull the journal at quarter end. Most teams start with the export and stop using it after two cycles.
How long does setup take?Registration is five short steps, and the register import is one paste. Statutory depth — TDS regime, PT states, weekly offs — is filled in later from settings and never blocks your first run.
