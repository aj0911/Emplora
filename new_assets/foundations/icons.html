Motion

Motion confirms a change, it never announces one. 110ms for hover and press, 180ms for anything that enters or leaves, one shared easing curve. Nothing in this product bounces, and nothing animates a number that a person is about to reconcile.
--dur-fast110msHover, press, focus ring
--dur-base180msDialogs, drawers, toggles, progress
--easecubic-bezier(.32,.72,.32,1)The only curve

Focus and keyboard

Every interactive element has a visible focus state: a 2px cobalt ring at 2px offset, or a 3px soft ring on filled controls. Tables are keyboard-navigable and the primary action of a wizard step is reachable with Enter.
Never remove the outline without replacing it.
