Rules

Do use cobalt for exactly one action per view. Do pick tone by meaning, not by mood. Do keep tinted fills at the 50 step so text on them stays readable at 13px.
Don't tint whole table rows except for selection. Don't introduce a second accent hue. Don't use pure black or pure white — the darkest ink is --neutral-950.
