The tenant is always visible

A payroll platform holds several companies' salary data at once. The org name sits under the brand in the sidebar on every screen, and platform-level (SuperAdmin) screens use a distinct ink topbar so there is never any doubt which side of the boundary you are on.

Permission is data, not an enum

Roles are created by the tenant, so nothing in the interface may hardcode four role names. Role pickers read the org's live list; the permission checklist is grouped by module with a select-all per group; the OrgAdmin role shows a Locked pill and no destructive action.

Editing a role that is currently assigned always says how many people it affects, before saving.

Destructive and irreversible actions

Approving a payroll run, suspending an organization and deleting a role are irreversible in practice. Each one states its blast radius in the confirming dialog, in figures, and the confirming button carries the verb — never "OK".

Sensitive data is gated and logged

Bank accounts, UANs and salaries are masked by default outside the screens that need them. Employees cannot silently edit their own bank details — they raise a correction request. Platform staff reach a tenant's granular data only through a time-boxed, reasoned support-access grant that appears in the audit log.

Nothing blocks day one

Registration asks only what a working account needs; statutory depth lives in settings. Optional-but-important data is surfaced later as attention items, not as required fields — a missing UAN is a badge on a row, not a blocked form.
